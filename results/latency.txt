Queue wait latency: noop jobs arriving at a steady rate
4 workers (16 slots), poll interval 200ms, 15s of arrivals per run, median of 3 runs
Wait = time from the job being created to its first attempt starting.
The first 2 seconds of each run are left out (warm-up).

  rate/s  achieved   p50 ms   p95 ms   p99 ms   max ms
     500       500    100.6    189.4    198.6    204.6
    1000      1000     25.9    147.3    173.6    195.2
    2000      2000      1.2      6.2     12.7     23.6
    3000      3000      2.4     12.5     20.7     26.5

Jobs are submitted one by one through the same code the API uses.
When the queue is empty, a worker sleeps for the poll interval, so a
new job can wait for that sleep to end. A shorter poll interval lowers
the wait and raises the number of queries sent to the database.
